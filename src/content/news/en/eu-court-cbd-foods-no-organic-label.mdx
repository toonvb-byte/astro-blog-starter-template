## What happened

In France, the national institute for origin and quality labels (INAO) told organic certifiers that products containing CBD (cannabidiol, an extract of the hemp plant) could not be certified organic. Its only exception was hemp oil not enriched with CBD. A hemp trade association, the Syndicat professionnel du chanvre, challenged this at the Conseil d'État, France's highest administrative court, which asked the EU Court of Justice for guidance (case C-495/25).

The association argued that CBD, as an extract of a farmed plant, counts as processed agricultural produce under the EU organic regulation (2018/848). The European Commission treats CBD as a novel food under Regulation 2015/2283 but has not authorised it as food in the EU.

On 5 November 2026 the Court held that CBD **cannot be certified organic as long as it is not authorised** for the EU market. An organic logo promotes a product and suggests quality and approval, so granting it to a food that may not be sold could put health and consumers at risk. The same applies to unauthorised products made for export. The Conseil d'État now has to apply the ruling.

## What it means for you

- **An organic logo on a CBD oil or gummy sold as food in the EU is a red flag.** Under this ruling such products cannot be certified organic while CBD has no novel food authorisation, and the Commission has not granted one so far.
- **"Organic" says nothing about safety or legality.** Safety is checked in the novel food authorisation, which requires that a food poses no safety risk on the available evidence and does not mislead consumers. The organic label covers how a product is made. What a label must show is in [our guide to reading supplement labels](/en/guides/how-to-read-eu-supplement-label/).
- **Plain hemp oil was not the issue.** The French exception covered hemp oil not enriched with CBD; the ruling is about CBD itself.
- **In Great Britain** the judgment is not binding. What the planned UK-EU food deal could mean for CBD there is in [our summit item](/en/news/uk-eu-summit-sps-supplements/).
