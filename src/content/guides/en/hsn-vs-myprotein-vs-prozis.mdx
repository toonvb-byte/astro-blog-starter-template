## The short answer

There is no permanently "cheapest" brand: prices for the same product change a lot from week to week. What you can do is **compare them properly**: price per gram of active ingredient, real dose per serving and shipping costs, on the day you buy. All three are large brands with their own manufacturing and stated certifications; none may promise more than EU law authorises.

## Who is who

| | HSN | Myprotein | Prozis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Country | Spain (Peligros, Granada) | United Kingdom | Portugal (Esposende) |
| Founded | 2007 | 2004 | 2006 |
| Ownership / founder | Founded by Andrés Sánchez | THG plc since 2011 | Founded by Miguel Milhão |
| Manufacturing | Own plant in Granada (since 2018) for dry products; liquids and creams at partner plants | Own facilities in the UK, according to the brand | Own factory in Portugal (per 2015 press) |
| Stated certifications | IFS Food (96.18% in 2024), GMP, HACCP, organic | ISO 9001, GMP, HACCP, BRC; athlete-verified range | Not verified by us |
| Published pricing policy | "Fair price": moderate margin, direct sales, limited advertising | Not published | Not published |

Manufacturing and certification details come from the brands themselves or the press, not from our own audits.

## Why the tub price misleads

**1. Prices swing a lot.** A real example: the **1 kg tub of HSN Creapure creatine** sold for **€70.11** at one online shop on 17 October 2026, while the lowest price recorded by the comparison site Kelkoo was **€53.31 on 2 September** — 24% less for the same product a few weeks earlier.

**2. Each brand recommends a different serving.** For creatine:

| Brand | Recommended serving | Actual creatine per serving |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HSN (Creapure) | ½ scoop = 3.4 g monohydrate | 3.0 g — exactly what the EU claim requires |
| Myprotein (unflavoured monohydrate) | 1 scoop = 3 g monohydrate | about 2.6 g |

If you compare "price per serving", HSN looks more expensive only because its serving is larger. The fair comparison is **price per gram of monohydrate**. Our [creatine guide](/en/guides/creatine-monohydrate-guide/) explains why 3 g of monohydrate is not 3 g of creatine.

**3. Shipping counts.** Delivery costs and free-shipping thresholds change with promotions. A cheaper tub plus €4–5 shipping can cost more than a slightly pricier one shipped free.

## How to compare in 3 steps

1. **Price per gram of active ingredient:** total price (with shipping) ÷ grams of active product in the pack. For protein, use grams of protein, not grams of powder.
2. **Same raw material:** compare Creapure with Creapure, and generic monohydrate with generic.
3. **Same day:** check all three on the same day; promotions often change the order.

## What no brand may promise

Whatever their size or seals, supplements in the EU may only use [authorised health claims](/en/guides/eu-approved-health-claims-supplements/). For creatine, the general one is that it increases performance in short, high-intensity efforts at 3 g a day. "Guaranteed muscle gain" or "fat burning" are not authorised.
