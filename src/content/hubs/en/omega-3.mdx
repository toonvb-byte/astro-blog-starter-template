  - >-
    For general use, **250–500 mg of EPA + DHA a day** matches the authorised claims.
    Higher doses are for specific purposes and best discussed with a doctor.
  - >-
    Prefer products with **independent testing** for oxidation and
    contaminants (for example IFOS) and a clear best-before date.
  - >-

    DHA.
  - >-

    closed, cool and dark; throw away capsules that smell rancid.

faq:
  - q: How much omega-3 should I take a day?
    a: >-
      If you rarely eat oily fish, a supplement giving 250–500 mg of EPA + DHA
      a day matches what the authorised claims are based on. EFSA considers up to 5 g
      a day safe for adults.
  - q: Is fish oil or algae oil better?
    a: >-
      Both work. Fish oil usually gives more EPA per euro; algae oil is vegan
      and raised blood DHA as much as fish oil in a comparison trial, but many
      algae oils contain little EPA.
  - q: Is triglyceride (TG) omega-3 better than ethyl ester (EE)?
    a: >-
      Re-esterified triglyceride forms may be absorbed somewhat better,
      especially without food. Taking any form with a meal narrows the gap;
      the EPA + DHA dose matters more.

## Omega-3 sources and forms compared

| Type | What it is | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish oil, triglyceride (TG) | Natural form of the oil | Moderate EPA + DHA per capsule |
| Fish oil, ethyl ester (EE) | Concentrated; the fatty acids are bound to ethanol | Cheapest concentrates; take with a meal |
| Re-esterified triglyceride (rTG) | Concentrated, then turned back into TG | High dose per capsule, higher price |
| Krill oil | Omega-3 partly bound to phospholipids | Low EPA + DHA per capsule; expensive per gram |
| Algae oil | From cultivated microalgae | Vegan; often mostly DHA |

## What the evidence says

If you eat oily fish (sardines, mackerel, salmon, anchovies) once or twice a week, you probably reach the reference intake already. A supplement makes most sense if you don't. The authorised claims for heart, brain and vision are based on modest daily amounts of 250 mg; marketing for memory, mood or joints goes beyond them.

Marine oils sold in the UK and the EU must meet limits for dioxins and PCBs. Our [fish oil vs algae oil guide](/en/guides/omega-3-fish-oil-vs-algae-oil/) compares the two sources and shows how to work out the cost per gram of EPA + DHA.
