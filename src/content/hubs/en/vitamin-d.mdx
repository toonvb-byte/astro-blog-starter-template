faq:
  - q: How much vitamin D should I take a day?
    a: >-
      For most adults, 10–20 µg (400–800 IU) a day in winter is enough. EFSA's
      adequate intake is 15 µg a day and its safe upper level is 100 µg from
      all sources. A 25(OH)D blood test is the only way to know if you are low.
  - q: Is vitamin D3 better than D2?
    a: >-
      Yes, for raising blood levels. A meta-analysis found D3 more effective
      than D2 at increasing serum 25(OH)D.
  - q: Do I need vitamin K2 with vitamin D?
    a: >-
      No. Vitamin D works without K2, and the EU has no authorised claim for the
      combination. K2 can interfere with some blood thinners.
  - q: Is 4,000 IU a day too much?
    a: >-
      4,000 IU (100 µg) is EFSA's safe upper level for total intake, not a
      target. It is five times Germany's guidance for a single supplement and
      above the legal limit in several EU countries.

## Vitamin D forms compared

| Form | What it is | Good to know |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D3 (cholecalciferol) | The form your skin makes; usually from lanolin (sheep's wool) | Raises blood levels more than D2 |
| Vegan D3 | D3 made from lichen | Same molecule, suitable for vegans |
| D2 (ergocalciferol) | Plant or fungal origin | Less effective at raising blood levels |
| Drops in oil | D3 dissolved in oil | Easy to adjust; check µg per drop |
| D3 + K2 | Combination with menaquinone (MK-7) | No authorised claim for the combination |

## What the evidence says

Your skin makes vitamin D from sunlight, but between roughly October and March the sun in most of Europe is too weak for that. In Germany's national health survey, **61.6% of adults** were below 50 nmol/l; in Spain, studies found clearly lower levels after winter than after summer. Older people, people with darker skin and people who rarely get sun are most at risk.

A daily supplement corrects low levels reliably. Whether it does much more than that in people who are not low is far less clear. Read [vitamin D in a European winter](/en/guides/vitamin-d-winter-europe/) for who needs it, and [D3 with K2](/en/guides/vitamin-d3-with-k2/) before paying extra for a combination.
